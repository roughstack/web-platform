# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of technical
familiarity.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and patient in technical discussion.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Respect project boundaries, security embargoes, and contributor privacy.
- Accept maintainers' decisions on scope while remaining free to disagree
  respectfully.
- Give credit for others' work and disclose meaningful conflicts of interest.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate
disruption, or publication of private security information is unacceptable.

## Enforcement

For ordinary moderation concerns, contact the maintainers through a private
GitHub channel. For security-sensitive or privacy-sensitive conduct, use the
private vulnerability-reporting path described in `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers
may remove content, restrict participation, or ban contributors when necessary
to protect the community and project.

Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact, prior behavior, and the
need to protect affected people. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical.
